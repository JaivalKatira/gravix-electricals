WHO WE ARE

THE STRENGTH
BEHIND EVERY CONNECTION.

Precision manufacturing. Quality engineering. Global reach.

Gravix Electricals is an Indian manufacturer of high-quality electrical connectors and grounding solutions, based in Rajkot, Gujarat. We serve the evolving needs of power distribution, utilities, infrastructure and industrial sectors.

From high-grade bronze and copper alloys to aluminium components, our products are designed for reliable electrical connections, mechanical strength, corrosion resistance and long-term performance.
